ABOUT US

Founded in 2016 under the visionary leadership of Zohaib Khan, AL-RAHEEM ASSOCIATES (AR Builder & Developers) has evolved into one of Karachi's premier property brokerage, civil contracting, and construction development institutions. Operating from Keamari Town, we specialize in high-yield commercial investments, residential planning, and turn-key construction execution.

With nearly a decade of active presence across Karachi's core real estate markets, we provide comprehensive real estate advisory for apartment bookings, showroom resales, warehouse godowns, and commercial lease acquisitions. Our compliance-first framework guarantees verified documentation and risk-free investments.

Beyond brokerage, our dedicated construction workforce—including master masons, steel fixers, MEP technicians, and structural finishing artisans—ensures every civil project meets rigorous seismic safety, thermal efficiency, and contemporary aesthetic standards.
