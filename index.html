ABOUT US

Meet Our Founder

Zohaib Khan

Owner • AL-RAHEEM ASSOCIATES

I am the Owner of AL-RAHEEM ASSOCIATES (and M/s Al-Raheem Associates), a trusted name in real estate, construction, and investment services established in 2016 in Karachi. We specialize in comprehensive rental and purchasing services, operating on a transparent commission basis to assist clients in finding suitable commercial, industrial, and residential properties.

In addition to real estate, we offer full-scope construction services, mobilizing a seasoned workforce of experienced masons, carpenters, certified electricians, and plumbers to ensure high-quality workmanship for residential and commercial construction needs. With a commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction, we strive to deliver reliable and professional turnkey civil solutions.

We also offer lucrative investment opportunities in real estate, booking of apartments & retail shops, resale of flats, shops & corporate offices, leasing of godowns/warehouses, and accurate property assessments. Headquartered at Azan Centre, Keamari, Karachi, our enterprise maintains a steady monthly income and verified corporate standing, certified by the Karachi Chamber of Commerce & Industry (KCCI #150586), FBR (NTN #5357401-7), and Pakistan Businessmen Association (BP-2670).

Our Vision

To be acknowledged as Karachi's most transparent and dependable partner in property investments, real estate acquisitions, and quality civil construction.

Our Mission

To deliver enduring structural excellence through skilled craftsmanship, honest commission-based client guidance, and verified financial transparency.
